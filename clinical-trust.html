About the Doctor

2D Echocardiography

Ultrasound scan to see how your heart pumps and valves work.

TMT (Stress Test)

Treadmill test to check heart function during exercise.

Holter Monitoring

24-hour heart-rhythm tracking for hard-to-catch symptoms.

Cardiac Consultation

Cardiac Health Packages

Device Therapy (AICD/CRT)

Radial Intervention

Wrist-route procedures — less pain, faster recovery.

Balloon Valvuloplasty

Widening narrowed heart valves without open surgery.
